Household Calendar

Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Household Calendar is a personal household application operated by Donald Ludemann. Contact: don803@gmail.com.

Information accessed and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the application reads events from the owner's primary Google Calendar and uses Gmail read-only access to search for calendar submissions sent to a designated email alias. Although that Gmail permission technically allows mailbox reading, the application's intake is limited by its search and sender/recipient checks. It processes relevant subjects, bodies, sender and delivery information, receipt times, message identifiers, and authentication results to create and validate local calendar entries.

Storage and display

Account credentials, tokens, local event records, source material needed for validation, extracted proposals, and review/audit records are stored on the owner's Linux computer outside the publicly served calendar directory. Accepted event details are displayed on the household network. People and devices with access to that display may see those details. Calendar information is not published on this public information website.

AI processing

Valid structured submissions are handled by a deterministic parser. For ordinary-language submissions from approved senders, the application uses Hermes and an OpenAI model through the owner's configured OpenAI integration. Relevant subject/body text and date context are transmitted to that provider to extract proposed calendar events. The extraction does not require unrelated mailbox content or account credentials. Ambiguous proposals are retained for local review. Provider processing and retention are governed by the applicable account settings and provider terms; this application does not promise zero provider retention.

Other services

Google supplies Calendar and Gmail APIs and a public holiday feed. GitHub Pages hosts this informational website and may collect ordinary service logs under GitHub's privacy statement. These public pages contain no application analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts.

Use and sharing limitations

Google user data is used to provide the household calendar and its event-extraction and review features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally supplied by the operator to train generalized AI models. Household Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Local events, submissions, cached proposals, and audit records remain until the operator removes them; there is no automatic retention deadline. The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account security settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase existing local records or previously transmitted provider data. Contact the operator to request correction or deletion of locally held data; provider-side requests are subject to the provider's controls and policies.

Changes

This page will be updated when material data practices change.
